01 / WHO WE ARE

Policy is a part of patient care.

The Student Network for Health Policy is a student-led organization founded by medical students at UT Southwestern Medical Center.

Our mission is to advance access to healthcare through legislative awareness, community education, and informed civic engagement. We study proposed policies, meet with public officials, and work toward practical workshops that help communities make their voices heard.

SNHP is a student organization. Content on this site represents student-led educational work and does not constitute an official position of UT Southwestern Medical Center.
